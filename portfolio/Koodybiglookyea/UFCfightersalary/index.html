The Evolution of the Octagon

The Evolution

From its brutal beginnings at UFC 1 in 1993 to becoming a multi-billion dollar global powerhouse under TKO Group Holdings, the UFC has redefined combat sports. The 2026 transition to the Paramount+ broadcast era marked the final step in turning MMA into a mainstream seasonal sport.

Hall of Fame Icons

Jon Jones

The consensus GOAT. Undefeated Heavyweight and Light Heavyweight champion with a record 16 title fight wins.

Amanda Nunes

The "Lioness" retired as the first woman to hold and defend two titles simultaneously.

Georges St-Pierre

The pioneer of the "complete fighter" era, mastering wrestling and striking in tandem.
